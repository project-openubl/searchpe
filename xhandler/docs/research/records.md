# xhandler-service: records, reporting, retention and purchase-side rules (Peru / SUNAT)

Research date: 2026-11-13. Sources were downloaded to `/tmp/sunat-regs/records/` (factura negociable material is in `/tmp/sunat-regs/records/fn/`) and extracted with `pdftotext`. Two sources were image scans: Ley 29623 (2010 text), Ley 30308 and RS 211-2015/SUNAT. I read their page images directly, which the PDF reader allows, and marked the quotes that come from them. Other scans could not be read at all, so they are listed under "Unverified" at the end.

"Requirement for xhandler-service" lines are design conclusions I drew from the cited text. They are not quotes.

## 1. SIRE (RVIE and RCE) and the status of PLE 14.1

### 1.1 Legal basis and structure
- **RS 000112-2021/SUNAT** (30/07/2021, in force 01/11/2021) created the **RVIE module** (Registro de Ventas e Ingresos Electrónico). Art. 2 says it can be reached through SUNAT Operaciones en Línea (SOL), through the "aplicativo cliente" desktop app, and through the **"servicio web API SUNAT"**. Generating or annotating the register itself happens **only through SUNAT Operaciones en Línea** (art. 2 b; art. 8.3, "Generación de registros"). Under art. 2 c), SUNAT stores, files and keeps the RVIE on the taxpayer's behalf.
- **RS 000040-2022/SUNAT** (24/03/2022) created the **SIRE**, made up of the RVIE and RCE modules (art. 1-A, inserted into RS 112-2021). It added the **RCE module** (art. 2-A), the RCE steps (art. 8-A), RCE post-period adjustments (art. 9-A) and Annexes 8–13. Under art. 5 b), as amended, a "generador" must generate the RVIE and the RCE **together** ("conjuntamente").
- **RS 000138-2023/SUNAT** (30/06/2023) changed how the RVIE proposal is complemented and replaced (art. 8.1.1, 8.2.1 b–c of RS 112-2021). The proposal can be **complemented**, but only through the on-screen form. When the proposal is **replaced**, the replacement may be "uno o más archivos planos". Online form loading is **not available through the API** ("salvo cuando se utilice el servicio web API SUNAT").

### 1.2 Who is obligated, and since when (latest text found)
The schedule is art. 3.1 of RS 112-2021 as replaced by RS 040-2022. It was then postponed by RS 190-2022, 000204-2023, 000258-2023, 000063-2024, 000145-2024, 000293-2024, 000217-2025, 000392-2025 and **000125-2026**, and amended by **000075-2026**:
- a) The taxpayers listed in Annex 7 (pilot group). Their date was moved to the July 2023 period (RS 190-2022).
- c) RER/MYPE Tributario taxpayers already obligated to keep electronic registers on 30/09/2023: from the **October 2023** period (RS 000204-2023).
- d) RER/MYPE taxpayers obligated on 31/07/2024: from **August 2024** (RS 000145-2024).
- e) Others obligated on 31/12/2024 that are **not principales contribuyentes**: from **January 2025** (RS 000293-2024).
- g) **Principales contribuyentes** designated as of 31/12/2024 (RS 000392-2025, as amended by RS 000125-2026):
  - g.1) net income of 2 300 UIT or less in fiscal year 2024: from **January 2026**;
  - g.2) net income above 2 300 UIT: from **October 2026** (RS 000125-2026, in force 01/07/2026).
  - A transitional rule in RS 000125-2026 let the g.2 group keep using SLE-PLE until the **September 2026** period. In that case the PLE file name carries indicator "1" at position 30.
- f) Taxpayers who become obligated between 01/01/2025 and 31/05/2026: whichever comes first of the third month after the obligation arises, the period in which they choose to become electronic issuers, or **June 2026** (RS 000075-2026 art. 5.1).
- h) Taxpayers who become obligated **from 01/06/2026**: from the period in which the obligation arises (RS 000075-2026 art. 5.2, in force 01/06/2026). Art. 1 b) of that RS states that everyone obligated to keep the IGV sales and purchase registers "**sin excepción**" must keep them in SIRE.
- Art. 4 also allows voluntary affiliation. Affiliation is definitive: "no procede la desafiliación".
- The SUNAT 2026 index lists resolutions up to 000227-2026 (13/11/2026). None of them postpones SIRE beyond RS 000125-2026.

Requirement for xhandler-service: treat SIRE as the **mandatory channel for every tenant** that keeps IGV sales and purchase registers, for periods from October 2026 at the latest and from June 2026 for most. Do not build a new-period PLE 14.1 / 8.1 export as a compliance feature.

### 1.3 What the taxpayer must do (workflow)
- **Proposal**: from the **2nd calendar day of each month**, SUNAT publishes the RVIE proposal for that month as a flat file. It is built from the comprobantes, notas, informative declarations and comunicaciones de baja received from issuers, and is refreshed daily (RS 112-2021 art. 8.1.1). The RCE proposal works the same way and also includes documents generated by SUNAT, such as type 54 and DAM import documents 50/52/53 (art. 8-A.1.1; RS 040-2022 Annex B, Table 11 note 1).
- **Preliminary**: from the **8th calendar day of the following month** (the 10th if the proposal belongs to an issuer of electronic utility receipts, recibo SP), the generator may do one of the following (art. 8.2.1 for RVIE, art. 8-A.2.1 for RCE):
  - a) **accept** the proposal;
  - b) **complement** it (RCE: add information, add allowed documents, or **exclude** documents other than credit notes);
  - c) **replace** it with a flat file (RVIE: Annex 3; RCE: Annex 11).
  - Non-domiciled purchase documents go in a separate file (Annex 9).
  - In the RCE, documents 01, 07, 08, 23, 30, 34, 42, 50, 52, 53 and 54 can be complemented only if their issue date is before the period being generated. Anything else requires replacing the proposal (RS 040-2022 Annex B, Table 11 note 2).
  - The RCE preliminary has two files: non-domiciled documents and everything else (art. 8-A.2.2).
- **Generation**: done only through the SOL "Generación de registros" option. It produces files containing a timestamp and a hash, and puts a **constancia de recepción** signed by SUNAT in the taxpayer's buzón (art. 8.3).
- **No activity in a period**: send an empty flat file (art. 6 c).
- **Post-period adjustments** ("ajustes posteriores"): RVIE uses Annex 4, RCE uses Annex 12. Adjustments to registers kept outside SIRE (manual, PLE or Portal) use RVIE **Annex 5** and RCE **Annex 13** (art. 9.3, 9-A.3).
- **CAR (Código de Anotación de Registro)**: 27 characters = issuer RUC (11) + document type (2) + series (4) + number (10), zero-padded (RS 112-2021 Annex, Table 7). Table 8 explains how to correct an entry whose CAR fields were wrong: void the old entry with amounts set to 0.00 and add a new one.

Requirement for xhandler-service: the service already holds the issued and received XML/CDR, so it should:
- (i) download the SUNAT proposal;
- (ii) reconcile it against its own records using the CAR key;
- (iii) offer "accept" when they match and "replace" (an Annex 3 or Annex 11 file) when they don't;
- (iv) produce adjustment files (Annex 4 or 12);
- (v) store the constancia de recepción, or remind the user to download it, because final generation can only be done in SOL. This last point is my reading of art. 8.3. Section 1.5 lists a "registrar preliminar" API service, so a hands-on check is needed.

### 1.4 File formats
- RVIE flat files are pipe-separated ("|"). Free-text fields must not contain `|`, `/` or `\`. Negative amounts are written as "- #.##" (RS 112-2021 Annex, Table 5).
- **RVIE file names** (Table 6): `LE` + RUC(11) + AAAA + MM + `00` + book `140400` + CC + O + I + M + `2` [+ NN] + `.TXT`.
  - CC: 01 accept, 02 replace, 03 adjustments, 04/05 adjustments of periods before SIRE (general / simplified format).
  - O (operations indicator): 0 RUC deregistered, 1 operating, 2 book closed.
  - I: 1 = file has content, 0 = empty.
  - M (currency): 1 = PEN, 2 = USD.
  - G: always 2.
- **RCE file names**: `LE…0008040001OIM2.TXT` and following (book 080400), and `…080500…` for non-domiciled (RS 040-2022 Annex B, Table 13).
- Physical receipts loaded to complement the RVIE: `RUC-CPF-AAAAMM-NN.txt`. Purchase CP import: `RUC-CP-AAAAMM-correlativo.txt`. Exchange-rate import: `RUC-RCETCA-AAAAMM-correlativo.txt`.
- In the RCE proposal, field 38 "Marca de operación sujeta a Detracción" is referential only and is not used to build the txt (Annex 8 as in RS 040-2022 Annex C; RS 000138-2023 Annex VII amends Annex 8 and I did not re-check it).

Requirement for xhandler-service: implement generators for RVIE Annex 3/4 and RCE Annex 11/12 (and 9 for non-domiciled), following the naming rules above. Validate field rules against the latest annexes (RS 112-2021 annexes as amended by RS 000138-2023 Annexes I–IX, which I did not read field by field).

### 1.5 SIRE API (documented)
- The norm is RS 112-2021 **Annex 6**: REST service; **POST** a ZIP containing a single file plus its **SHA-256** hash, which returns a ticket; **GET** the ticket for a JSON status plus the "Reporte de información recibida" and an error file; **OAuth 2.0/JWT** obtained "utilizando las credenciales y su clave SOL". Credentials are created in SOL. Requesting new credentials disables the old ones (art. 7.3 a).
- The official manuals are "Servicios Web API – SIRE Ventas" **v25 (05/06/2025)** and "SIRE Compras" **v25 (05/06/2025)**, at cpe.sunat.gob.pe. The PDFs downloaded without problems; the HTML pages on that host return 403.
  - Credentials: in SOL, "Credenciales de API SUNAT", register the application, select the URI "MIGE RCE y RVIE - SIRE", and you get a client_id and client_secret.
  - Token: `POST https://api-seguridad.sunat.gob.pe/v1/clientessol/{client_id}/oauth2/token/` with `grant_type=password`, `scope=https://api-sire.sunat.gob.pe`, client_id, client_secret, `username="{RUC}{USUARIO SOL}"` and `password={CLAVE SOL}`.
  - Base URL: `https://api-sire.sunat.gob.pe/v1/contribuyente/migeigv/libros/rvierce/...`.
  - Ventas services (§5.1–5.37) include: list periods (codLibro 140000), replace-proposal upload, accept proposal, register preliminary, add/delete documents, download proposal/summary/inconsistencies/RVIE/constancia de recepción, ticket status, download file.
  - Compras services (§5.1–5.61) add: non-domiciled loads, include/exclude documents, exchange rate, prorrata, crédito fiscal reintegro, ajustes posteriores.
  - Uploads use TUS metadata (filename, perTributario, codOrigenEnvio=2, codProceso, nomArchivoImportacion following the Table 6 names).

Requirement for xhandler-service:
- Per tenant (RUC), store a client_id and client_secret **plus a SOL user and password**, because SIRE tokens use the password grant. Use a dedicated secondary SOL user with limited profile, store the secrets encrypted, and rotate them.
- Build an async ticket-polling worker.
- Keep the service version-aware: the manual is at v25 and changes often.

### 1.6 Deadlines
- **RS 281-2022/SUNAT** sets the maximum-delay dates ("fechas máximas de atraso") for the electronic sales and purchase registers "para el año 2023 y siguientes". Art. 6 points to Annex II, which explicitly covers registers kept in SLE-PLE, SLE-Portal "**o SIRE**".
  - Deadline for period M, by last digit of the RUC: digit 0 → 10th business day of M+1; 1 → 11th; 2–3 → 12th; 4–5 → 13th; 6–7 → 14th; 8–9 → 15th; buenos contribuyentes and UESP → 16th.
  - I found no later resolution changing Annex II. RS 000257-2025 only changes the delay for books filed with Formulario Virtual 0625.
- Register generation per RS 112-2021 art. 6 b): once per period, "dentro o fuera del plazo", and only "periodo tras periodo", meaning in order.

Requirement for xhandler-service: build a per-tenant SIRE calendar from the last RUC digit and the business-day calendar, with alerts. Block generating period N+1 before period N.

### 1.7 Is PLE 14.1 still relevant?
- No, for current periods. RS 000075-2026 requires SIRE "sin excepción", and the last group (g.2) moves to SIRE from October 2026 (RS 000125-2026). The PLE affiliation rule (RS 286-2009 art. 5.1 a, as amended by RS 040-2022) allows affiliating for the Annex 1 registers only up to the September 2022 period. RS 286-2009 art. 11 requires closing the PLE sales and purchase registers before generating them in SIRE.
- Adjustments to periods kept in PLE are now filed **through SIRE** (RVIE Annex 5, RCE Annex 13; file codes CC = 04/05).
- PLE remains for other books, such as the Libro Diario (RS 040-2022 approved PLE 5.4).

Requirement for xhandler-service: drop PLE 14.1 as a primary requirement. Optionally keep it, or Annex 5 output, only for historical adjustments of pre-SIRE periods.

## 2. Retention and conservation of XML and CDR

### 2.1 Código Tributario (TUO DS 133-2013-EF, consolidated text by SUNAT "normas modificatorias al 26.6.2026")
- **Art. 87 numeral 7**, wording from DL 1372, 2018. Books, registers, "documentos y antecedentes de las operaciones" must be stored, filed and kept for "**cinco (5) años o durante el plazo de prescripción del tributo, el que fuera mayor**". The 5 years count from 1 January of the year after the filing deadline of the related return. For income-tax prepayments, the count starts from the annual return deadline.
  - Loss or destruction must be reported within **15 business days**.
  - "Cuando el deudor tributario esté obligado o haya optado por… emitir de la manera referida [electrónica]… la SUNAT podrá sustituirlo en el almacenamiento, archivo y conservación de los mismos", and may also substitute "los demás sujetos que participan en las operaciones".
- **Art. 87 numeral 8**: systems and storage media holding taxable-base data must be kept in working order for the same period (5 years or the prescription period, whichever is greater).
- **Art. 87 numeral 5**: the taxpayer must exhibit documents and files and provide access profiles to its electronic data system during audits.
- **Art. 87 numeral 4**: the taxpayer must tell SUNAT where books, registers and electronic storage media are kept.
- **Art. 43** (prescription): 4 years; 6 years if no return was filed; 10 years when a withholding or perception agent did not pay over what it withheld or perceived. **Art. 44** sets when the count starts (1 January of the following year, depending on the case).

Requirement for xhandler-service:
- Default retention: **at least 10 years** for XML, CDR, comunicaciones de baja, resúmenes and their tickets. That covers the 10-year case for agents. The norm minimum is 5 years from 1 January after the filing deadline, extended to 6 years where no return was filed. Make retention configurable per tenant, and never purge while a tenant has an open audit.
- Keep documents readable and exportable for the whole period (art. 87.8).
- Support emergency export for audits (art. 87.5).

### 2.2 SEE-OSE (RS 117-2017/SUNAT, original 2017 text; later amendments only partly checked)
- **Art. 12.5**: "La SUNAT sustituye al emisor electrónico en la obligación de almacenar, archivar y conservar aquello que emita en este sistema", **except** boletas and their notes when the issuer reports them through a resumen diario. The issuer may download copies from SUNAT Virtual.
- **Art. 29.1.1**: the issuer must keep:
  - a) copies of boletas and linked notes when it uses the resumen diario;
  - b) **a copy of every CPE and nota it receives as an electronic acquirer** ("adquirente o usuario electrónico").
- **Art. 29.1.2**: a non-electronic acquirer keeps the printed representation or the copy when it has tax effects.
- **Art. 29.1.3**: storage may be on magnetic or optical media, "entre otros".
- **Art. 29.2**: when boletas are not sent individually, the issuer must make them available to the acquirer electronically for **one year** from issue, with authentication so only the acquirer can access them. **Art. 33.4** sets the same one-year duty with authentication for CRE/CPE (retention and perception receipts).
- **OSE duties, art. 7**:
  - 7.5: send each CDR and the checked document to SUNAT **within one hour**;
  - 7.6: **99.96%** yearly availability;
  - 7.7: **ISO/IEC 27001** certification from the second year, with yearly audit reports;
  - 7.9: secure web-service channels with username and password;
  - 7.10–7.11: tax secrecy and confidentiality;
  - **7.12: "Mantener por un mes el documento electrónico respecto del cual se emitió una CDR, así como las CDR y las comunicaciones de inconsistencias"**.

Requirement for xhandler-service:
- **As OSE**: keep the checked XML, CDR and inconsistency notices for at least 1 month (art. 7.12). The tenant-side retention in 2.1 still applies to the issuer's own archive.
- **As issuer platform**: SUNAT's substitution covers what was **sent to SUNAT/OSE**. It does not cover boletas reported only through resumen diario, and it does not cover the issuer's **received** documents. Those must be archived by the tenant, or by the platform on its behalf.
- Provide an authenticated acquirer portal or download for boletas, kept for at least 1 year.

### 2.3 SEE-Del contribuyente (RS 097-2012/SUNAT) and PSE (RS 199-2015/SUNAT)
- I could not read the RS 097-2012 PDF (scan), so its conservation article is **unverified**.
- RS 199-2015 (PSE registry), text layer partly OCR'd:
  - **Art. 3** lists the activities a third party may perform only if registered as PSE: (a) issuing facturas, boletas and notas; (b) sending copies to SUNAT; (c) generating and sending bajas, resúmenes diarios and resúmenes of printed receipts; (d) receiving the constancias de recepción. The PSE does this using the issuer's certificate and/or SOL credentials. Under art. 4-A of DL 25632 the issuer **stays responsible**.
  - **Art. 6**: PSE obligations are support, **ISO/IEC 27001**, and allowing SUNAT inspection.
  - Neither article lists storage or conservation as a PSE activity.

Requirement for xhandler-service: as PSE, register in the PSE registry for the art. 3 activities and maintain ISO 27001. **Storage by a third party (PSE, OSE or cloud) is not prohibited** by the norms I read: CT 87.7 regulates the duty and its duration, not where the data is kept, and RS 117-2017 art. 29.1.3 allows any media. The taxpayer stays responsible, though, and must be able to exhibit the documents (art. 87.5) and report where they are kept (art. 87.4). Contracts should therefore guarantee export on exit and survival of the archive if the tenant leaves or the service ends.

### 2.4 SIRE registers
- Under RS 112-2021 art. 2 c) / 5 c) (as amended by RS 040-2022), SUNAT replaces the generator in storing the RVIE and RCE.

Requirement for xhandler-service: the registers themselves need not be archived. The **constancias de recepción**, replacement and adjustment files and reconciliation evidence should be archived, since they support the books.

## 3. Purchase side: received invoices, validity checks, crédito fiscal and RCE

### 3.1 Duties of the acquirer
- An electronic acquirer must **keep a copy** of each CPE and nota received (RS 117-2017 art. 29.1.1 b) for the CT 87.7 period.
- Under RS 117-2017 art. 18.1.1, a factura is "otorgada" when delivered or made available electronically. It is "a disposición" once deposited at the e-mail address the acquirer designated, or once the issuer enables download on its website (art. 18.4). When a printed copy is also handed over, compliance is checked against the electronic document (art. 18.5 b).
- If the copy is lost, the acquirer can download it from SUNAT's website with clave SOL for facturas (art. 31.1 a).
- **Validity of what was received**: a factura or nota sent to SUNAT/OSE after the deadline does **not** count as a factura electrónica, "aun cuando hubiera sido entregada al adquirente" (RS 117-2017 art. 15.1). The deadline was 1 calendar day after the issue date under RS 193-2020 and became **3 calendar days** under **RS 000003-2023** (SEE-Del contribuyente art. 12.1; SFS art. 12.2; OSE art. 15.1). DAE and GRE have 7 days.

### 3.2 How validity is checked
- RS 117-2017 art. 30.1.1 says SUNAT Virtual offers a lookup of validity, issuance conditions and minimum requirements for facturas, DAE, linked notas and GRE, and art. 30.2 requires clave SOL for it.
- **"Consulta Integrada de Comprobante de Pago por Servicio Web"** (SUNAT manual v2, at cpe.sunat.gob.pe):
  - Credentials: in SOL, "Empresas / Comprobantes de pago / Consulta de Validez… / Credenciales de API SUNAT", register the app, get an Id and Clave.
  - Token: `POST https://api-seguridad.sunat.gob.pe/v1/clientesextranet/{client_id}/oauth2/token/` with `grant_type=client_credentials` and `scope=https://api.sunat.gob.pe/v1/contribuyente/contribuyentes`. No SOL password is needed.
  - Query: `POST https://api.sunat.gob.pe/v1/contribuyente/contribuyentes/{RUC consultante}/validarcomprobante` with JSON `numRuc`, `codComp` (01, 03, 04, 07, 08, R1, R7), `numeroSerie`, `numero`, `fechaEmision` (dd/mm/yyyy), and `monto` (only for electronic documents).
  - Response fields: `estadoCp` (0 NO EXISTE, 1 ACEPTADO, 2 ANULADO, 3 AUTORIZADO, 4 NO AUTORIZADO), `estadoRuc` (00 ACTIVO … 22), `condDomiRuc` (00 HABIDO, 12 NO HABIDO, …) and `observaciones`.
- I found **no norm** (resolución de superintendencia) that regulates this API. It is documented only in the manual.

Requirement for xhandler-service: for each supplier XML a client uploads:
- (i) verify the XMLDSig signature and the UBL/XSD structure;
- (ii) call Consulta Integrada with the tenant's own client credentials (client_credentials grant) and store the response with a timestamp;
- (iii) flag ANULADO, NO EXISTE, or a supplier RUC that is not ACTIVO/HABIDO. IGV art. 19 b) ties crédito fiscal to the issuer being "habilitado" according to SUNAT's public data;
- (iv) cross-check against the RCE proposal (§1.3), which is SUNAT's own list of what was received.

### 3.3 Is receiving or keeping the XML required for crédito fiscal or the RCE?
- **TUO Ley IGV art. 19** (formal requirements, per SUNAT's consolidated text):
  - a) IGV shown separately on the comprobante;
  - b) issuer name and RUC consistent with SUNAT's public data, and the issuer **habilitado** to issue on that date (wording from DL 1540, 2022);
  - c) the document **recorded in the Registro de Compras**. Late or defective compliance with the register's formal duties does not cause loss of crédito fiscal (Ley 29566 paragraph).
  - Holding the XML file is **not** stated as a separate requirement.
- **DU 013-2020 art. 8.2** (Title I): for facturas in credit transactions, crédito fiscal may be used in the period the factura is recorded in the Registro de Compras "siempre que se otorgue la conformidad de la factura y de la información adicional". Its Fourth Final Complementary Provision applies this to IGV obligations arising from the 1st day of the 4th month after the Reglamento took effect. The Reglamento is DS 239-2021-EF, in force 18/09/2021 per RS 000165-2021. My own computation from that date gives January 2022. I have **not verified** whether this crédito-fiscal condition is still in force (see §4 and Unverified).
- **RCE**: the proposal is built by SUNAT from issuers' submissions (RS 040-2022 art. 8-A.1.1). The acquirer can accept it without holding the XML. The XML is still needed for the conservation duty (§3.1), for audits, and to check what the proposal shows.

Requirement for xhandler-service:
- Make XML storage of received documents part of the archive (5+ years, see §2).
- Treat the RCE proposal, rather than XML possession, as the trigger for the purchase register.
- When the tenant is the acquirer, track **conformidad status** of credit facturas (§4), because DU 013-2020 may make crédito fiscal depend on it.

## 4. Factura negociable

### 4.1 Laws
- **Ley 29623** (07/12/2010; scan read visually). Original art. 3 listed the minimum content; original art. 7 gave **8 business days** to accept or contest.
- **Ley 30308** (12/03/2015; scan read visually) amended arts. 2 and 5–8:
  - recognized the representation of the factura negociable **by anotación en cuenta** in an ICLV (Institución de Compensación y Liquidación de Valores, i.e. CAVALI);
  - kept 8 **business** days for conformity, with a presumption of conformity when the period ends;
  - told SUNAT to set the mechanisms for electronic issuers.
- **DL 1178** (2015) is cited by RS 211-2015. I did not read it.
- **RS 211-2015/SUNAT** (13/08/2015; scan read visually), art. 3:
  - 3.1: a supplier may include in the electronic factura the data in "literales b), c) y d) del artículo 3 de la Ley". Issuers on SEE-Del contribuyente use the UBL standard.
  - 3.3: the ICLV can check the validity of an electronic factura through a SUNAT lookup service, available from 01/10/2015.
- **DU 013-2020** (23/01/2020; full text from El Peruano PDF). Title I came into force 10 months after publication; its Reglamento is **DS 239-2021-EF**, in force 18/09/2021.
  - **Art. 6.1**: issuers of facturas or recibos por honorarios **for credit transactions** must include, on the issue date, **(a) the agreed payment term (plazo de pago acordado) and (b) the net amount pending payment (monto neto pendiente de pago)**.
  - **Art. 6.2**: when payment is late, the supplier records the actual payment date on a PRODUCE platform.
  - **Art. 6.3**: the factura must be made available to the acquirer **and SUNAT** within **2 calendar days** of issue.
  - **Art. 7.1**: the acquirer has **8 calendar days** (no longer business days), "por única vez, no prorrogable", from that availability date to give conformity or non-conformity. **7.2**: the period is extended by 2 days when the non-conformity, or the response to it, is entered on the last day. **7.3**: silence means **irrevocable presumed conformity**. **7.4**: everything is recorded **on the platform SUNAT provides**. **7.5**: if a non-conformity is not resolved, a credit note, debit note or new comprobante starts a new process.
  - **Art. 8.1**: the only grounds for non-conformity are payment term, net pending amount, or a complaint about the goods or services.
  - **Art. 9.1**: unpaid amounts accrue compensatory and moratory interest at the BCRP maximum rates.
  - **Art. 10**: an electronic factura carrying the art. 6 information may be **registered (anotación en cuenta) in an ICLV with or without conformity**, after the ICLV validates the data **with SUNAT**. The ICLV notifies the acquirer, and the 8-day period does not restart.
- **DU 013-2020, First Complementary Modifying Provision**, rewrote Ley 29623 arts. 2, 3, 3-A, 4, 6, 7 and 8:
  - **New art. 3-A** (factura negociable from an electronic CPE) requires at least: literal b) supplier signature and domicile (for electronic documents, the CPE signature, clave SOL, or an e-signature accepted by the SMV); c) acquirer domicile; d) due date; **e) "monto neto pendiente de pago"**; **f) payment date(s), single or in installments**. SUNAT must provide the mechanism to include these data and a copy that can be sent to an ICLV.
  - **New art. 8**: an electronic factura negociable can be transferred **once it is registered with an ICLV**. The holder must report the actual payment date to the ICLV within **1 business day** after payment.
  - **New art. 7**: for **printed** facturas negociables, 8 **calendar** days from the presentation record.

### 4.2 SUNAT implementation: RS 000165-2021/SUNAT (in force 17/12/2021)
- Creates the **conformity platform** within SEE/SOL, reachable from SOL and through an **API REST** (OAuth/JWT with client credentials plus clave SOL; ZIP + SHA-256 + ticket, as in Annex II).
- Scope: facturas in credit transactions with an acquirer identified by RUC (art. 1.9, 1.26).
- **Availability** (art. 3.3): SEE-Del contribuyente, SFS and OSE facturas are made available **on the date the accepted CDR is issued**. For State entities, the next day (art. 3.4). SUNAT notifies the acquirer's buzón (art. 3.8).
- **Conformity period** (art. 4.1): **8 calendar days**, +2 days if the action falls on the last day. Non-conformity grounds and supporting evidence (art. 4.2 b); the issuer is notified through its buzón (art. 4.4).
- **Fixing a non-conformity** (art. 5): the issuer issues a **nota de crédito** that corrects the description, the net pending amount, or the payment date(s) and installment amounts, or that answers the complaint, and then selects that NC on the platform or through the API. If the period ends unresolved, an "NCE que inicia el nuevo proceso" restarts the process (art. 6).
- Unusable documents: facturas annulled by NC or given de baja (art. 3.6).
- Batch API file names (Annex I): `RUC-{PND|PNR|SUB|DIS}-YYYYMMDD-NN.txt`.
- The resolution also amended RS 097-2012 art. 22, RS 117-2017 art. 26 and SOL norms so a credit note can change the "64-A / 64-B" items (pending amount and installments). In the validation rules, this is **NC type 13**, which may only target a credit factura.

### 4.3 XML fields (local SUNAT validation rules, AjustesValidacionesCPE v2026-02-12, `_Factura2_0.md` items 174–177; RS 193-2020 for SEE-SOL)
- `cac:PaymentTerms` with `cbc:ID`=`FormaPago` and `cbc:PaymentMeansID` = `Contado` | `Credito` | `Cuota001…`. Mandatory since 01/01/2022 (errors 3244–3248).
- For `Credito` with an RUC acquirer:
  - **monto neto pendiente de pago** in `cbc:Amount` (error 3251); it must be ≤ PayableAmount (3265) and equal the sum of the installments (3319);
  - at least one `CuotaNNN` (3249), each with `cbc:Amount` (3254) and `cbc:PaymentDueDate` later than the IssueDate (3256, 3267).
- RS 193-2020 (SEE-SOL text) defines the net pending amount as **excluding IGV withholdings, the SPOT deposit and other deductions**.
- Credit note type `13`: only for a factura (3259), and the factura must be a credit factura (3260). It may modify only one factura (3261).

Requirement for xhandler-service:
- (i) Always populate FormaPago, the net pending amount and installments correctly for credit sales, computing net = total − IGV withholding − detracción − other deductions.
- (ii) Show the conformity status for each issued credit factura, and for received ones when the tenant is the acquirer. Do this through the SUNAT conformity API (RS 000165-2021 Annex II; the API manual itself was not found).
- (iii) Support NC type 13 to fix amounts and dates during the 8-day window, and the "NCE que inicia nuevo proceso".
- (iv) Let the user act on conformity within 8 calendar days (+2), with reminders.
- (v) For factoring, export the XML, CDR and conformity evidence. Registration in CAVALI is done by the supplier or factor through CAVALI's procedures, with CAVALI validating the data with SUNAT (DU 013-2020 art. 10.3). I found no rule requiring the invoicing platform to send anything to CAVALI itself.

## 5. Detracciones (SPOT), percepción and retención: what the invoice and the registers must carry
Source: local validation rules, `_Factura2_0.md`. I did not read the SPOT norm (RS 183-2004/SUNAT).
- **Detracción**, for operation types 1001–1004:
  - `cac:PaymentTerms` with `cbc:ID`=`Detraccion`, `cbc:PaymentMeansID` = good/service code from catálogo 54 (3127, 3033; type 1002 → 004, 1003 → 028, 1004 → 027 per 3129), `cbc:Amount` = detracción amount in **PEN** (3035, 3208), and `cbc:PaymentPercent`;
  - `cac:PaymentMeans` with `cbc:ID`=`Detraccion` and **`cac:PayeeFinancialAccount/cbc:ID` = the supplier's Banco de la Nación account** (3034), plus PaymentMeansCode from catálogo 59.
- **Percepción**: operation type 2001; `PaymentTerms` `Percepcion` holding the total including perception, in PEN (3308–3311, 2788).
- **IGV withholding** (items 178–180): `cac:AllowanceCharge` with ChargeIndicator `false`, reason code `62`, base, rate and amount.
- **Registers**:
  - RCE proposal field 38 marks operations subject to detracción, for reference only.
  - The **constancia de depósito** fields (issue date and number) and the "sujeto a retención" flag appear only in **RCE Annex 13**, which is for adjustments to purchase registers kept outside SIRE (RS 040-2022 Annex H, fields 32–34 / 24–26). There the deposit date and number are optional when a linking system can identify the comprobantes (note 6).
  - I did not see these fields in the current RCE Annex 8/11 extract, but I did not read those annexes field by field.

Requirement for xhandler-service:
- Issue side: emit the detracción PaymentTerms and PaymentMeans (with the BN account) and the IGV-withholding AllowanceCharge, and subtract both from the net pending amount for credit sales (§4.3).
- Purchase side: let users record the constancia de depósito number and date for each received factura, needed both for the crédito fiscal timing under SPOT (not verified here) and for legacy adjustments.
