# Regulatory research

Peruvian regulations that shape xhandler-service. Researched on 2026-11-13 from
official sources: SUNAT's legal indexes and PDFs, El Peruano, gob.pe, and SUNAT's
CPE validation rules v2026-02-12.

| Report | Covers |
|---|---|
| [roles.md](roles.md) | Emission systems (SEE-Del contribuyente, SEE-OSE), PSE (RS 199-2015 / 108-2022), OSE (RS 117-2017 and amendments), one company acting as both PSE and OSE |
| [documents.md](documents.md) | Sending and voiding deadlines, boleta rules, Reglamento de Comprobantes de Pago, contingency, GRE, percepción/retención |
| [records.md](records.md) | SIRE vs PLE, retention of documents, purchase side and validity checks, factura negociable, detracciones |
| [legal.md](legal.md) | Personal data (Ley 29733, DS 016-2024-JUS), digital signatures (Ley 27269, DS 052-2008-PCM), cybersecurity, consumer protection, taxes on the SaaS, data residency |

Caveats:

- Some base norms (RS 097-2012, 300-2014, 007-99, 274-2015) are image-only scans.
  Their current text was taken from the resolutions that amend them. Each report lists
  what could not be verified.
- These reports are engineering research, not legal advice. Confirm them with a tax
  and data-protection lawyer before relying on them commercially, and especially
  before the PSE and OSE registrations.
- Regulations change. Review this folder whenever SUNAT publishes new rules
  (see the 2027 changes in `documents.md`).
